A few details about the stadium and its unique experience.

The stadium is located on Avenida de Concha Espina in Madrid, Spain.

Its renovation introduced a striking new exterior, a retractable roof, advanced pitch technology and a panoramic 360-degree video display.

Stadium tour availability and access can vary depending on events and operational arrangements. Check the official Real Madrid website before planning your visit.
